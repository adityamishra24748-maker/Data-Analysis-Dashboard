# 📊 Student Performance Analysis & Early-Warning Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard designed to analyze the academic performance of 500 students, identify students who may be at risk, and understand the key factors associated with student performance.

## 🎯 Project Objective

> **“30% of our students fail every year. We have data on 500 students. Can we identify who is at risk — and why — before it’s too late?”**

This project uses **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), correlation analysis, factor analysis, and student risk segmentation** to transform raw student data into actionable insights for teachers and school administrators.

* 🎯 Principal-focused recommendations

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

The analysis of the 500-student dataset revealed:

* **500 students** analyzed
* **12 students** were classified as failing in the supplied dataset
* Observed failure rate: **2.4%**
* **21 students** fall into the High/Critical early-warning categories
* Weekly self-study hours show a strong positive relationship with total score
* The correlation between self-study hours and total score is approximately **0.80**
* Risk segmentation helps identify students who may require additional academic support

> Note: The original problem statement assumes a 30% failure rate, but the supplied dataset shows an observed failure rate of 2.4%. The dashboard reports the actual dataset results rather than forcing the data to match the assumption.

## 🎓 Business/School Recommendations

Based on the analysis, schools can:

* Monitor students with consistently low self-study hours.
* Provide targeted academic support to high-risk students.
* Create regular early-warning reports for teachers.
* Encourage structured study routines.
* Monitor student performance over time.
* Use risk indicators for **support and intervention**, not punishment.
* Review intervention outcomes regularly.
